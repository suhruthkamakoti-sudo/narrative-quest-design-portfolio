## Design Post-Mortem: Process Rationale

### What Didn't Work
* **Front-Loaded Exposition:** Early iterations relied on opening NPC dialogue to explain the mythology of the Dula Orb and the Sorceress. In pacing tests, this removed player agency and stalled the forward momentum expected in an open, living world.
* **Premature Mechanical Friction:** High-density combat during the initial dive distracted from the spectacle of the cataclysm, creating unnecessary fail-states before core swimming/stamina loops were established.

### What Did Work
* **Sensory Pacing (Audio/Visual Cues):** Anchoring state transitions to sharp physical beats (`BOOM!`, `PLOP!`, `GRAB!`, `WOOOSH!`) gave clear triggers for camera impulse shakes, particle drops, and audio transitions.
* **Contained Ethereal Encounters:** Placing the Sorceress confrontation inside an instantaneous mental pocket delivered high-stakes narrative exposure without requiring early-game boss rigging or breaking open-world continuity.

### Why
In single-player games with MMO-style exploration, motivation must stem from active discovery rather than passive exposition. Narrative weight is sustained when lore is earned through physical, playable action.
